# SPF, DKIM and DMARC

## SPF
Sender Policy Framework helps prevent email spoofing by identifying authorized mail servers.

## DKIM
DomainKeys Identified Mail validates message integrity using cryptographic signatures.

## DMARC
Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting and Conformance combines SPF and DKIM policies and provides reporting.

## Why They Matter
These technologies help reduce phishing, spoofing, and email-based attacks.
