**Risk Calculations**

  Single Loss Expectancy - SLE - AV * EF   --> {AV = Asset Value; EF - Exposure Factor} 
  Annual Loss Expectancy - ALE - SLE * ARO
  Annual Rate of Occurrence - ARO 
  
**Controls**

  Administrative Controls: People & process
    EG: Policies, Background Checks, Security Awareness Training, Job Retention
    
  Technical Controls: Technology enforcing security
    EG: Firewalls; MFA; IDS/IPS; Encryption; EDR
    
  Physical Controls: Protect physical assets
    EG: Locks, Guard, Fences, CCTV, Badge readers, mantraps

**Governance Documents**    

  Policy --> Most Important --> Entire company must be protected
  Standard --> Technical Requirements --> Endpoint must run approved EDR softwares
  Procedure --> Step by step --> steps to install an EDR
  Guideline --> Recommendation --> Recommended EDR tuning settings
