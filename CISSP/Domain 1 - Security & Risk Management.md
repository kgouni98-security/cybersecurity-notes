
**Lesson 1: The CISSP Mindset**

**(CIA, Governance, Risk, Risk Responses)**

The CISSP exam usually rewards the management-level answer before the technical answer.

**Core Concept 1: CIA Triad**
The foundation of information security.

**Confidentiality**

Only authorized users can access data.

Examples:

Encryption
Access controls
DLP

**Integrity**

Data remains accurate and unmodified.

Examples:

Hashing
Digital signatures
File integrity monitoring

**Availability**

Systems remain accessible when needed.

Examples:

Redundancy
Backups
Disaster recovery

**Access Principles**

Least Privilege
Separation of Duties
Need to Know

**Governance**

Policy → Standard → Procedure → Guideline

**Risk Responses**

Accept
Mitigate
Transfer
Avoid
